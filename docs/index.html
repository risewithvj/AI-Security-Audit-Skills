Why it matters now

  • The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025, with core obligations phasing in by about May 2027.
  • Penalties for failing to protect personal data can reach ₹250 crore.
  • Breaches must be reported to the Data Protection Board; the detailed report is due within 72 hours.

How to run it

  • Web: pick "DPDP only" in the prompt studio, or Pro/God (DPDP runs automatically when personal data is found).
  • CLI: /ai-dpdp-audit, which reuses the security run's data map.
  • Technical readiness only. Not legal advice, and it never says "compliant".

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is my code safe?

The prompt sends nothing anywhere. Your zip goes only to the AI you choose, under that AI's own terms. Never zip .env files or real secrets. The CLI runs on your machine and saves reports outside your project.

Does it fix my code?

No. It gives recommendations only, with the smallest safe fix for each finding. You or your developers decide what to change.

Why "no proof, no finding"?

AI reviews often pad reports with weak "issues". Here every vulnerability needs the attacker, the path with file and line, the missing check and the result. Unproven items go to "Needs check".

Free AI or paid AI?

Free plans have small limits, so use Lite for a quick top-risk check. Paid plans can run Pro, God Mode or the Deep 4-pass pack for a full audit. The CLI goes deepest.

What is DPDP?

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, with the DPDP Rules, 2025. The DPDP audit checks notice, consent, safeguards, breach readiness, retention and erasure, rights, children's data and transfers, in your actual code. It's technical readiness, not legal advice.

Which AI is best?

In our test, Claude.ai and Codex scored highest among web AIs, ChatGPT gave the most careful reports, and Gemini Pro refused. The CLI scored more than twice any single web AI.

Does this page track me?

Only anonymous usage, and only if you click Accept. Google Tag Manager runs with Consent Mode v2, so storage starts denied and advertising stays off. Your audit content is never tracked.
